OpenrowDB **0.2.1** is a small patch after the v0.2.0 maturity release.

### Fixed

- **Postgres rows affected** — `UPDATE`, `DELETE`, and `INSERT` statements now report affected-row counts in the query results pane and history (MySQL already did; Postgres was pending a PostgresNIO metadata path).

### Changed

- **ROADMAP** — documents Tier A (v0.2.0 sprints A–H) and Tier B pre-v1 backlog.
